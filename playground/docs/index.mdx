# rspress-plugin-typst

Write documentation pages in [Typst](https://typst.app/) and render them through Typst's experimental HTML export inside [Rspress](https://rspress.rs/).

## Why Typst?

- Familiar typesetting for technical writing
- First-class math and structured content
- Compiles through a Rust-powered Node-API binding

## Try it

Open the [Hello Typst](/guide/hello) page — it is authored as `guide/hello.typ`.
